How WebSleuth AI Works

Multi-layer AI analysis running on IBM LinuxONE for maximum accuracy and security.

1

URL Capture

Browser extension intercepts every URL you visit before the page loads. Zero delay to your browsing experience.

2

Feature Extraction

16 URL signals extracted: domain similarity, subdomain depth, special chars, HTTPS, suspicious keywords, and more.

3

AI Risk Scoring

Our heuristic AI engine scores the URL 0–100 for risk. Trained on thousands of phishing patterns and improving daily from community reports.

4

Registry Verification

URL is checked against our IBM LinuxONE-hosted signed registry of 28+ verified government portals.

5

Instant Alert

You see a green ✅ or red ⚠️ warning in milliseconds. Fake sites get blocked with a redirect to the real portal.

6

Community Learning

Every user report updates the AI model in real-time. WebSleuth AI gets smarter with every use — a true living AI.
